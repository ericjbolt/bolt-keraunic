# ⚡ Bolt Keraunic

**Open the app: [ericjbolt.github.io/bolt-keraunic](https://ericjbolt.github.io/bolt-keraunic/)**

A clean, free weather app built on open data. No ads, no subscription, and no account required.

*Keraunic* (adjective): relating to lightning. The keraunic number is the count of thunderstorm days a place has each year.

## Features

- Current conditions with twelve detail tiles, including dew point, visibility, air quality, and ultraviolet index
- Next 24 hours, hour by hour
- Moon phase, illumination, moonrise and moonset, and the next full and new moon
- 7-day forecast with precipitation type, chance, amount, and an hourly timing strip for wet days
- Thunderstorm risk within 15 miles, with a link to live lightning strikes
- National Weather Service alerts for United States locations
- Live global maps for wind, temperature, rain, ocean waves, smoke, dust, carbon monoxide, and sulfur dioxide
- Search by city or United States ZIP code, or use your current location

## Add it to your iPhone

1. Open [ericjbolt.github.io/bolt-keraunic](https://ericjbolt.github.io/bolt-keraunic/) in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Data sources

- Forecast, air quality, and city search: [Weather data by Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/) (CC BY 4.0)
- Alerts: [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/)
- ZIP code lookup: [Zippopotam.us](https://zippopotam.us/)
- Moon calculations: [SunCalc](https://github.com/mourner/suncalc)
- Live lightning: [Blitzortung.org](https://www.blitzortung.org/)
- Global maps: [earth.nullschool.net](https://earth.nullschool.net/)

Forecasts are provided for general information only. For official watches and warnings, consult the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/).
